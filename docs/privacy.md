Effective date: 27 September 2026. Publisher: QortxAI.

Photo Metadata Repair Inspector processes the folders you choose on your computer.
It reads file paths, file sizes, filesystem timestamps and available photo date
metadata to display inspection results. Repairs change selected files only after
you review the proposed changes and explicitly confirm them.

The application contains no account sign-in, advertising, analytics or photo-upload
feature. Application code does not send your photos, metadata or inspection results
to QortxAI. Windows and Microsoft Store services may handle installation, updates
or diagnostic information under their own privacy settings and policies.

When backups are enabled, first-original copies are stored in
`.photo-repair-backups` inside the scanned folder. Repair attempts are recorded in
`.photo-repair-repair-log.csv`, including file paths, operations, before/after values,
backup paths where applicable and status. These artifacts remain on your computer;
you control their retention. Uninstalling the app does not remove these files from
your photo folders or undo repairs. Disabling backups prevents creation of the
app's original-file recovery copy for those repairs.

If you choose a network or cloud-synchronized folder, its provider may transfer
files, backups or logs according to that service's configuration. The app itself
does not provide cloud synchronization.

If you submit a support request through GitHub, the information you choose to post
is handled by GitHub and may be public. Do not post personal photos, sensitive
metadata or private file paths. Describe the issue using disposable example files
where possible.

Questions can be submitted through the project's
[support tracker](https://github.com/abulhawa/Windows-Photo-Repair-Inspector/issues).

## Public policy and website visitors

Public policy: https://qortxai.com/projects/photo-metadata-repair-inspector/privacy/.
For privacy questions, email qortxai@outlook.com. Information you choose to send
by email is processed to respond to your request through the email service.

The local-processing statements above describe the desktop application. The
website is hosted by Vercel and loads its font stylesheet from Google Fonts;
visiting it involves requests to those services. Other pages on qortxai.com,
including the homepage, include Vercel Web Analytics. Those website services are
separate from the app and do not receive your photo collection through the app.
